The two moves that track the toolchain bump, both predicted by the folds' own notes: **`patra-open-close` +7.1%** (patra 1.16.0 probes for `<db>.wal` and runs WAL recovery before every statement, so a crashed writer's journal is no longer truncated) and **`sha256-64B` +5.8%** (sigil 3.13.11's one-shot wipes its context; the fixed cost shows at 64 B and vanishes by 1 KiB). Everything else is flat. 1.7.0's own changes (token binding, conflict tracking) are off these paths. Raw numbers: [`2026-10-19-v1.7.0.csv`](2026-10-19-v1.7.0.csv).
