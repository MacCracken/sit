# Benchmarks — 2026-10-19 (v1.7.3)

cyrius **6.7.15** for both builds. 1.7.2 and 1.7.3 were measured interleaved on a quiet host (load average under 1). The previous snapshot is [`2026-10-19-v1.7.2.md`](2026-10-19-v1.7.2.md). Deltas under ~3% are noise.

## History walks — the commands 1.7.3 changed

On a 2,000-commit linear history, with a tag 9 commits below the tip (DCE builds, average of 3–5 runs):

| operation | 1.7.2 | 1.7.3 | note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `describe`, linear | 2 ms (`near-9`) | 1 ms (`near-9`) | exact fast path: a single-parent chain stops at the first tag |
| `describe`, one merge above the tag | 2 ms (`near-11`, wrong) | 151 ms (`near-12`) | needs the full ancestry for git's count; 1.7.2 missed the side commit |
| `rebase` of a 3-commit branch | 154 ms | 151 ms | the pending walk now covers upstream's ancestry instead of the branch's |

All three costs come from the commit-object read every history walk pays, about 75 µs per commit. A commit-parent cache is on the roadmap (1.x).
